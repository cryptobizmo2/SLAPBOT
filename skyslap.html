How to fly

  • ✥Drag anywhere to fly. Guns fire on their own. With 360° controls your jet faces where you fly and locks onto enemies when you stop. Standard keeps the nose pointing up.
  • Double-tap or hit the loop button to spin 360°. You can't be hit while you spin. 360° controls only.
  • BTap the red button for your plane's special. It also wipes out enemy fire.
  • PShoot red-striped planes for power-ups: P power, B bomb, W wingmen, + repair.
  • Collect $SLAPGOLD and earn XP. Level up to unlock new jets and weapons in the Hangar.
