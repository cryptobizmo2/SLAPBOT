Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This page explains what we collect when you use the SLAPBOT pages and play Big Mouf: Sky Slap, how ads and cookies work, and how to have your data deleted.

"We" means SLAPBOT, the team behind the SLAPBOT scanner pages, the SLAP IT N YA MOUF and BIG MOUF $SLAPGOLD pages, and the Big Mouf: Sky Slap game, all at cryptobizmo2.github.io/SLAPBOT. You can reach us by direct message on X at @slapitnyamouf.

What we collect

When you play Sky Slap online

The game gives you an anonymous pilot account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Our game server stores:

  • a random pilot ID and your callsign (you can change it)
  • your Slap Coins balance, XP, stars, unlocked missions, jets and upgrades
  • a record of each run (score, kills, coins and timing), so the server can stop cheating and run the leaderboard

If you connect a wallet

Connecting a Phantom wallet is optional. If you do, we store your public wallet address and your $SLAPGOLD balance, which unlock the holder jets and perks. You sign a free message to prove the wallet is yours. We never see your private key or recovery phrase, and signing never moves or spends tokens.

When you use the scanner and token pages

When you look up a token, the page asks our server and public data sources (DexScreener and Solana network providers) for public market and on-chain data about that token.

Technical logs

Like most websites, our hosting providers (GitHub Pages for the pages, Railway for our servers) keep technical logs such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request. These are used to run and secure the service.

What stays on your device

  • Local storage. The game saves your progress and settings (sound, controls, graphics) in your browser. The scanner pages save your preferences the same way.
  • A short-lived cookie. On iPhone, the game sets a first-party cookie named skyslap_h for up to 10 minutes. It holds a one-time code so the app you add to your Home Screen can pick up the same pilot. Nothing else is stored in it.
  • An offline copy. If you install the game as an app, your device keeps a copy of the game files so it opens fast and works offline.

We don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Ads and cookies

We may show ads in Sky Slap and on our pages from Google and other ad partners.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of other vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where the law requires it, you will be asked for your consent before ad cookies are used.

Optional ads in the game. Some ads in Sky Slap are your choice: watch one for a free revive or to double the Slap Coins from a run. Slap Coins are game coins for the Hangar. They are not the $SLAPGOLD token, have no cash value, and can't be withdrawn, sold or traded. Watching ads never pays you crypto or money.

Services we rely on

Each of these has its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages hosts our pages. Railway runs our game server and database.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on our pages.
  • DexScreener and Solana network providers supply public token data.
  • Phantom, only if you choose to connect your wallet.
  • Coinbase, only if you tap a Buy link, which opens Coinbase's site.
  • Google and other ad partners, when ads are shown.

How we use it

We use this information to run the game and the leaderboard, stop cheating, unlock holder perks, show ads and keep our services secure. We don't sell your personal information.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, message us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to remove everything stored on your device.
  • To delete your pilot account and its records, or to unlink your wallet, message @slapitnyamouf on X with your callsign. We will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Nothing on our sites is financial advice. Holding a token gives you access to perks, not a promise of value.
